# Yijia Sun

**Cloud microphysics · Ice nucleation · Aerosol–cloud interactions**

I am a PhD researcher in Atmospheric Sciences at Stony Brook University. I study how aerosol particles initiate ice formation in Arctic mixed-phase clouds, combining process-based modeling, observational constraints, and single-particle aerosol analysis.

My work focuses on ice-nucleation parameterizations, aerosol and ice budgets, and the interpretation of model–observation differences during marine cold-air outbreaks.

## Research highlight

**How do freezing parameterizations and entrainment shape cloud ice?**

## Selected research and software

### Aerosol–Cloud One-Dimensional (AC-1D) model

I contribute to the development and application of AC-1D, a Python model for investigating aerosol and ice-nucleating-particle evolution under cloud conditions informed by large-eddy simulations. My research uses the model to examine ice formation and aerosol–ice budgets in Arctic mixed-phase clouds.

[Explore the collaborative codebase](https://github.com/open-atmos/AC-1D)

### COMBLE aerosol–INP closure

With Daniel A. Knopf, I contributed the aerosol–INP closure tutorial to the COMBLE Model–Observation Intercomparison Project Cookbook. The notebook uses measured aerosol size distributions to compare predictions from different immersion-freezing parameterizations with observed INP concentrations at COMBLE and Zeppelin.
